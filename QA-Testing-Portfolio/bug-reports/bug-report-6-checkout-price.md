# 🐛 Bug Report — BR-006

## Title
Checkout page total price is incorrect

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Add 2 different products to the cart
2. Navigate to the **checkout page**
3. Review the displayed total price

---

## Expected Result

Total price correctly reflects the sum of all selected products.

---

## Actual Result

Total price shown is **higher** than the actual sum of the cart items.

---

## Severity

🔴 **High** — Overcharging users is a critical financial and legal issue.
